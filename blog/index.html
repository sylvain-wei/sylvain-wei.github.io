October 14, 2026 · Original on X

Neurons are plastic and remember every action you take. Delayed gratification is essentially about building immediate feedback for actions that bring greater rewards over the long term.

When you're at a low point, protecting yourself from being hurt is, of course, the safest thing to do. But you can't stay in that state forever, because self-protective behaviors often establish a short-term positive feedback loop. You have to try to break that loop.

神经是具有可塑性的，会记住你的每一个行为。延迟满足，本质上是去建立完成长期更有收益行为后的即时反馈。

人在低谷的时候，最安全的方式当然是保护自己不受创伤。但是不能总是沉溺于此，因为保护自己的行为往往建立了短期的正反馈机制，要尝试打破这种反馈。。
