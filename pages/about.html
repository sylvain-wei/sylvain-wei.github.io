About Me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at the Institute of Computational Linguistics (计算语言学研究所), School of Computer Science, Peking University, advised by Prof. Houfeng Wang.

You can also find me on Twitter/X and REDNote.

I am open to collaborations and discussions, and I am actively seeking research intern opportunities.

I am working on:

Foundation Model Alignment and Post-training
  • Analyze how RLVR shifts token distributions and turns sparse supervision into effective reasoning gains [Sparse but Critical]
  • Develop adaptive post-training signals and efficient reasoning regularizers [RankTuner] [GRSP]
Long-horizon Capability Generalization in Agentic Systems
  • Evaluate temporal reasoning and memory use in realistic long-context scenarios [TIME]
  • Study tool-augmented agent reasoning in medical decision workflows [MeNTi]
Real-World Data Synthesis and Construction
  • Construct a real-world temporal reasoning benchmark for evaluating LLMs across time-sensitive scenarios [TIME]
  • Scale repository-level coding data for software engineering agents [SWE-Ext]
  • Build evaluation signals for factuality, citation faithfulness, and human preference [Truthfulness] [CiteCheck] [MindScore]

If you share similar interests or would like to chat, feel free to reach out at shaohang@stu.pku.edu.cn.
