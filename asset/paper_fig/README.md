# Paper figure sources

The following publication previews are static exports of the authors' original
paper figures. They were recovered from the corresponding arXiv TeX source,
rendered from the supplied vector PDF with Poppler, and converted to WebP for
web delivery. No data, labels, legends, or scientific content were changed.

`ReVuE-2026.png` is the unchanged static overview from the
[ReVuE project repository](https://github.com/sylvain-wei/ReVuE/blob/main/assets/overview.png),
corresponding to [arXiv:2609.36838](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36838).

The previews remain subject to the licenses and attribution terms of their
respective papers; their inclusion here does not relicense the paper figures.
