Shaohang Wei

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Peking University, advised by Prof. Houfeng Wang. My research interests broadly span language modeling, with a particular focus on post-training, alignment, and reasoning. I strive to understand the learning behaviors of LLMs and push the boundaries of reasoning ability and generalization. You can also find me on Twitter/X and REDNote.
